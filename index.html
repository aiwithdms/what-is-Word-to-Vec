05 / Explain it to a beginner

“Think about teaching a computer a language. Humans understand words through experience and context. Computers work with numbers. Word embedding methods represent words as lists of numbers called vectors. By learning from many examples of text, a model can learn patterns that place words with related uses near each other in a numerical space. These representations can help AI systems with search, language tasks, and recommendations.”

06 / Remember the difference

Vector

A list of numbers representing a point or direction in a mathematical space.

[0.8, 0.6, 0.2]

Word embedding

A learned numerical representation of a word or token, designed to capture useful patterns and relationships.

Word → Vector
